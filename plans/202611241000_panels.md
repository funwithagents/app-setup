# The Setup and Secrets panels

**Status:** Done

Implements [specs/ui.md](../specs/ui.md) ("Using the panels", "The panels"), [specs/app.md](../specs/app.md) ("The model") and [specs/config-folder.md](../specs/config-folder.md) as revised: module-setup no longer draws a page or runs a session. An app draws the two panels where it wants in its own page and runs its sessions itself, telling the model when the config is in use.

## Scope

- `src/module_setup/session.py`, `specs/session.md`, `tests/test_session.py` — removed: the session is the app's.
- `src/module_setup/app.py` — `App` without `start` and `session_ui`; `AppModel(app, folder, secrets=None, locked=None)` ensures the folder and loads the `.env` it opens itself; `locked()`, `path`, `blockers` (was `start_blockers`), `used_config` (was `session_config`); no `start`, `stop`, `autostart`, settings.
- `src/module_setup/folder.py` — no `settings.json`, no `Settings`: an app keeps its own settings in the folder.
- `src/module_setup/ui/page.py` → `ui/panels.py` — `setup_panel(model) -> SetupPanel(draft, saved)` and `secrets_panel(model, config=None)`, each with its own state, events and page-load step; Save and Reset disabled while locked. No `build_page`, no `launch`.
- `src/module_setup/ui/report.py` — no session line.
- `tests/test_app.py`, `tests/test_folder.py`, `tests/ui/test_panels.py` (was `test_page.py`), `tests/ui/test_report.py`, `tests-e2e/test_panels_in_browser.py` (was `test_page_in_browser.py`).
- Specs, `_overview.md`, `_index.md`, `AGENTS.md`, `README.md`, `pyproject.toml` — the panels instead of the page.

## Steps

1. Remove the session runner and the settings; give the model its lock and its setup steps.
2. Split the page into the two panels; a panel's page-load step is `gr.on` with no trigger and no input.
3. Tests: the model's lock, blockers and setup steps; the panels drawn in an app's own tabs; the lock and the secrets' config through the registered handlers; in the browser, the panels in non-first tabs, the Secrets panel following the unsaved draft, Save disabled while a session runs.
